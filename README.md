# ProofLayer

### Building Verifiable Digital Infrastructure for Real-World Assets

ProofLayer is a platform designed to bridge the gap between real-world assets and digital infrastructure by creating verifiable, tamper-evident digital records for physical assets.

Our long-term vision is to make real-world assets more transparent, verifiable, transferable, and digitally accessible.

## 🌐 Vision

Real-world assets exist outside the blockchain, while digital ownership and records exist inside digital systems.

ProofLayer aims to build the verification layer connecting these two worlds.

We are building infrastructure that can:

- Verify real-world asset information
- Establish trusted digital asset records
- Preserve document and record integrity
- Create transparent asset histories
- Enable controlled digital transfers
- Anchor verified records to blockchain
- Support multiple real-world asset categories

## 🚀 Initial MVP

The first MVP focuses on **vehicles** as a practical real-world asset use case.

The MVP includes:

- User authentication
- Asset registration
- Vehicle information
- Document management
- Document integrity hashing
- Verification workflow
- Asset lifecycle tracking
- Ownership/event history
- Blockchain anchoring foundation

Vehicle is only the starting point. The architecture is designed to expand into other real-world asset categories.

## 🔐 Architecture

ProofLayer follows an off-chain/on-chain architecture.

### Off-chain

Sensitive and large data remains off-chain, including:

- Personal information
- KYC data
- Original documents
- Photos
- Inspection information
- Legal records

### On-chain

Blockchain can be used for:

- Asset identifiers
- Record hashes
- Verification anchors
- Transfer events
- Tamper-evident history

Sensitive personal documents should not be stored directly on-chain.

## ⚠️ Important

A blockchain record or digital token does not automatically constitute legal ownership of a physical asset.

ProofLayer is designed to work alongside applicable legal, regulatory, registration, and ownership-transfer processes.
